# MenuMint — Initial Acquisition Funnel

## Offer

**Restaurant Direct-Order Starter — ₹2,999 one-time**

A mobile menu page, QR code, direct WhatsApp order message, up to 40 items, and two updates in the first 30 days. The sales promise is deliberately limited: it does not claim more orders or a WhatsApp API integration.

## Funnel

1. A restaurant owner sees a personalised, manual outreach note or relevant marketplace listing.
2. The note leads to the live MenuMint page and sample menu.
3. The owner submits the “Request a sample page” form with a public menu link.
4. We return a branded preview built from their existing public menu.
5. If approved, the owner pays ₹2,999 through an approved payment method before the final QR assets and production link are supplied.
6. We deliver the page and QR PDF; post-delivery changes use the same request form.

## First test

- Target: 30 independent cafés/restaurants with a public menu but no usable mobile order path.
- Channel: individually reviewed public business contact forms or listed business email addresses; one relevant message only, no scraping, bulk messaging, or WhatsApp outreach without opt-in.
- Offer: a no-cost sample layout, not a discount.
- Decision threshold after 30 prospects: at least 3 substantive replies or 1 sample request means continue; otherwise revise the niche/offer before expanding.

## Message draft (do not send until the page is published)

Hi [Name], I noticed [specific, observable menu friction such as “your menu is a PDF”]. I build a simple mobile menu that customers open from a QR code and use to start a WhatsApp order directly with the restaurant. Here is a working sample: [demo link].

I can make a no-cost sample layout using your current public menu. If it looks useful, the fixed pilot price is ₹2,999; if not, no action needed. Would you like to see one?

— MenuMint

## Operating rules

- Do not send to personal contacts, employer contacts, or colleagues.
- Keep each outreach message specific, truthful, and limited to one follow-up at most.
- Immediately stop contacting a recipient who opts out or indicates no interest.
- Never state or imply affiliation with WhatsApp, Google, delivery platforms, or a restaurant.
