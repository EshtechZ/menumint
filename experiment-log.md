# 30-Day Micro-business Experiment Log

## Guardrails

- Goal: collect the first ₹10,000 in legitimate revenue from customers who do not know the founder, within 30 days.
- Budget: ₹0 unless the founder expressly approves a purchase.
- Acquisition: no paid ads, employer, colleagues, existing network, or personal contacts.
- Founder time: at most two hours per week; account creation, authentication, payments, and material decisions are escalated.

## Day 0 — 22 September 2026: discovery screen

Status: research complete; MVP deliberately not started pending founder review of the shortlist.

| Opportunity | Demand signal | Competition / concern | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| IndiaMART lead-response automation | A specialist competitor advertises ₹2,499/month and a seven-day free trial. | Needs a customer's portal/API access and ongoing messaging compliance. | Shortlist |
| Restaurant QR menu + WhatsApp order page | Fiverr shows 1,233 relevant services, including menu pages and ordering setups. | Commodity design-only offer; needs a measurable conversion angle. | Shortlist |
| Google Business Profile optimisation | Fiverr shows 8,023 GMB-profile services. | Extremely crowded and Google verification cannot be delegated. | Reject |
| Google Sheets dashboards | Fiverr shows 17,949 sheets-dashboard services. | Directly contrary to the generic reporting assumption; highly crowded. | Reject |
| Notion tracker/template | Fiverr shows 2,324 template services and 835 tracker services. | Requires volume/platform discovery; low willingness to pay. | Reject |
| Editable invoice/quotation kit | Fiverr shows 3,995 invoice-template services. | Commodity, legal/tax confusion risk, and low price ceiling. | Reject |
| Digital business card | Fiverr search shows only 44 services. | Lower competition but weak/one-off pain and low ticket. | Reject |
| Indian wedding e-invitation templates | One Etsy seller reports 1.7k sales and a 103-review shop; listings sell at roughly ₹679–₹3,332. | Marketplace-dependent; varied design tastes and platform/account onboarding. | Shortlist |
| WhatsApp Business catalogue setup | India-specific research says WhatsApp Business has a free catalogue, quick replies, labels, and click-to-chat. | Setup work is manual; many sellers already have a basic profile. | Reject / bundle feature only |
| Generic process automation | Upwork reports 269 client reviews for India-based process-automation specialists. | Broad scope, hard to explain/price without a niche. | Reject / niche only |
